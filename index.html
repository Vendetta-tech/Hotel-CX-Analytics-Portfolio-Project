From data to guest experience decisions

This project simulates how a Customer Experience or Data Analyst would structure a hotel analytics problem, investigate customer friction, and communicate recommendations to management.

ANALYSIS SNAPSHOT

What the guest data revealed

Four headline signals from the 286 simulated guest stays, translated into clear customer experience priorities.

3.64/5

Overall satisfaction

The portfolio's headline measure of guest experience health across the full stay journey.

45.8%

Complaint rate

Service friction is significant enough to make complaint prevention and recovery a clear priority.

74.8%

Complaints resolved

Most recorded complaints were resolved, but unresolved cases still represent a guest recovery risk.

29

Cleanliness complaints

Room Cleanliness was the highest-frequency complaint theme in the simulated dataset.
