# Hotel Customer Experience Analytics Case Study

## Executive Summary

The objective of this project is to demonstrate how a customer experience analyst can transform hotel guest feedback and operational data into actionable business decisions.

## Business Problem

Hotel leadership needs visibility into which customer segments experience lower satisfaction, which service touchpoints generate the most friction, and where operational resources should be prioritized.

## Analytical Process

### 1. Understand the business question

Define satisfaction, complaint, advocacy, and retention metrics before beginning analysis.

### 2. Prepare the data

Validate data types, missing values, duplicates, and inconsistent category labels.

### 3. Explore performance

Analyze satisfaction by customer segment, booking channel, room type, repeat status, and service touchpoint.

### 4. Diagnose pain points

Identify recurring complaint themes and unresolved service cases.

### 5. Prioritize actions

Use Impact × Frequency × Feasibility to rank improvement opportunities.

### 6. Communicate findings

Present the analysis through a concise dashboard and recommendations linked to measurable KPIs.

## Interview Explanation

“I built this project to simulate an end-to-end customer experience analytics workflow. I started with a business problem, validated and explored the data, defined CX KPIs, segmented customers, analyzed service pain points, and translated the findings into measurable operational recommendations. The data is simulated, but the analytical process is designed to mirror how a CX or business analyst would structure a real project.”

## Disclaimer

This is a simulated portfolio project and does not represent the performance or customer data of a real hotel company.
