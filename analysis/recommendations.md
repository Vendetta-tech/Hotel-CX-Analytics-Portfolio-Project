# Recommendations Framework

## Priority 1: Fix recurring high-volume pain points

Focus first on issues that combine high frequency with a strong relationship to low satisfaction.

**Potential actions**
- Standardize service recovery workflow
- Define response time targets
- Track unresolved cases daily

## Priority 2: Improve weak service touchpoints

Compare touchpoint ratings against overall satisfaction to identify operational bottlenecks.

**Potential actions**
- Targeted staff training
- Process redesign
- Peak-period staffing review

## Priority 3: Protect repeat guests

Monitor satisfaction and complaints separately for repeat guests because dissatisfaction among this segment can create disproportionate retention risk.

## Measurement Plan

Track each initiative using a baseline, target, owner, review frequency, and success metric.

| Initiative | KPI | Review |
|---|---|---|
| Service recovery | Resolution Rate | Weekly |
| Complaint reduction | Complaint Rate | Weekly |
| Touchpoint improvement | Average Rating | Monthly |
| Retention | Repeat Guest Rate | Monthly |
