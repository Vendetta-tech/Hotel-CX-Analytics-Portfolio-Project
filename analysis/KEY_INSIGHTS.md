# Key Insights

The following observations are derived from the simulated portfolio dataset.

## 1. Overall CX health

The dataset contains a mixture of highly satisfied guests and a concentrated group of dissatisfied guests with service complaints. This makes the dataset useful for demonstrating segmentation and root-cause analysis.

## 2. Complaint pattern

Recurring complaint categories include:

1. Room Quality
2. Cleanliness
3. Staff Service
4. Check-in Delay
5. Food & Beverage

The portfolio analysis should rank complaint volume and combine it with resolution status. High-volume unresolved issues should receive the highest management attention.

## 3. Guest segment pattern

The simulated dataset contains Business, Leisure, Family, and Solo guests. Analysis should compare both satisfaction and complaint rates rather than relying on overall averages.

## 4. Operational lesson

A low average rating alone does not explain what should be fixed. The recommended workflow is:

**Identify weak segment → identify weak touchpoint → inspect complaint category → check resolution status → recommend action.**

## 5. Recommended management priorities

- Reduce unresolved complaints
- Improve consistency of room and cleanliness standards
- Strengthen service recovery
- Review staff service performance during low-rated stays
- Monitor repeat guests separately as a retention-sensitive segment
