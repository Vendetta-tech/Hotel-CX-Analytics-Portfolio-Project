# Portfolio Story

## Situation

Hotel management wants to improve guest experience but has fragmented satisfaction and complaint information.

## Task

Analyze the available guest experience data and identify actionable opportunities.

## Action

I structured the analysis around CX KPIs, customer segmentation, service touchpoints, complaint categories, and resolution performance.

## Result

The project produces a repeatable framework for identifying weak experiences and connecting them to measurable operational actions.

## Important Interview Note

Because this is a simulated portfolio project, do not claim that the recommendations generated real financial results. Say instead:

“The analysis identified opportunities that I would recommend validating against real operational and financial data before implementation.”
